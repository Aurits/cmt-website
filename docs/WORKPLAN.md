## 1. Phase 1 overview

The current project focuses on developing and launching the new CMT Realtors website.

The website is being developed as a Next.js application, with the main pages, design, navigation
and responsive experience already in place.

As part of this phase, we will develop a custom CMS from scratch specifically for CMT Realtors.
The CMS will provide a central place for managing properties, images, website content and
enquiries.

This two-week period represents Phase 1 of the platform development. The focus is to establish a
strong working foundation, complete the core functionality, test the system and prepare it for the
initial launch.

---

## 2. Development plan

### Week 1 — Core application & CMS development

The first week will focus mainly on completing the core application and developing the CMS.

**Main activities**

**Next.js application**

- Complete the remaining website pages and functionality.
- Refine the responsive experience across desktop and mobile.
- Review navigation, layouts and content.
- Prepare the application for production.

**Custom CMS**

- Develop the CMS from scratch.
- Create secure access for CMT users.
- Develop property management functionality.
- Add property creation, editing and publishing.
- Add image upload and management.
- Add management of website content and market updates.

**Forms & enquiries**

- Develop property enquiry and valuation forms.
- Connect enquiries to the CMT team.
- Configure automatic email responses.
- Test the complete enquiry process.

**Initial testing**

- Test features as they are completed.
- Identify and resolve issues before the final review.

> **Week 1 target.** Have the main website, CMS and core management functionality working
> together.

---

### Week 2 — Testing, review & launch preparation

The second week will focus on bringing the complete system together, testing it and preparing it
for the final launch.

**Main activities**

**Full system testing**

- Test the website across desktop and mobile.
- Test property creation, editing and publishing.
- Test image uploads.
- Test forms and enquiries.
- Check navigation, content and major user flows.
- Fix issues identified during testing.

**CMT review**

- Provide CMT with access to a private preview.
- Review the website and CMS with the CMT team.
- Collect feedback and make the agreed final changes.
- Confirm the final version for deployment.

**Final configuration**

- Complete production configuration.
- Confirm hosting, email and other required services.
- Configure Google and analytics requirements.
- Prepare backups and recovery procedures.
- Confirm the required CMT information and access.

**Domain replacement & launch**

The replacement of the current website on **cmtrealtors.com** will take place during the final
stage of the project.

This will only happen after CMT has reviewed and confirmed the new website for launch. The final
stage will include:

1. Confirming the final website and content.
2. Confirming the required production access and accounts.
3. Completing the production setup.
4. Deploying the new application to cmtrealtors.com.
5. Performing final checks on the live website.
6. Confirming that forms, enquiries and CMS functions are working correctly.
7. Training the CMT team and completing the handover.

> **Week 2 target.** Have the website reviewed, confirmed, deployed and ready for CMT to use.

## 3. Information & access required from CMT

The following information and access will be required during the development and final launch
stages:

| Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Professional registration details | Complete professional information |
| Uganda and Kenya business structure | Confirm how the businesses should be presented |
| Priority services | Determine the services to highlight |
| Team photographs | Complete the team section |
| Approved client information | Complete the client section |
| WhatsApp / mobile number | Connect communication features |
| Office hours | Display accurate business information |
| Property information and photographs | Populate the property section |
| Domain, hosting and email access | Complete production setup and deployment |

*The earlier the required information and access are provided, the easier it will be to complete
the final review and launch without delays.*

## 4. Phase 1 deliverables

At the end of Phase 1, the following core components are expected to be available:

- Complete website.
- Custom CMS developed specifically for CMT.
- Property management functionality.
- Property image management.
- Website content management.
- Working enquiry and valuation forms.
- Email notifications and responses.
- Responsive website for desktop and mobile.
- Production deployment on cmtrealtors.com.
- Google and analytics configuration.
- CMS training and handover.

---

## 5. Notes: future development

Phase 1 establishes the foundation of the CMT digital platform.

The platform can be expanded in future phases with more advanced features as CMT's requirements
grow. Some of the expected future areas include:

- Digital listing and management of properties.
- Digital signing of forms and documents.
- Property ownership verification.
- Advanced property search and filtering.
- Additional client-facing features.
- More advanced property and business management functions.

*These features are not part of the current two-week Phase 1 scope. They can be planned and
developed as separate phases based on CMT's priorities and requirements.*

---

## 6. Phase 1 completion

Phase 1 will be considered complete once the core platform has been developed, tested, confirmed
by CMT and successfully deployed.

`Development` → `Testing` → `CMT Review` → `Confirmation` → `Production Setup` → `Launch` →
`Training & Handover`

> **Phase 1 goal.** To deliver a stable, manageable and scalable digital foundation for CMT
> Realtors, with the core website and CMS ready for immediate use and further development in
> future phases.
