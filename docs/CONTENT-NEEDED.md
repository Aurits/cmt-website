# CMT Realtors website: what we need from you

A short checklist of the information and photographs we need to finish the website.

> This checklist is also available as a Word document: `docs/CMT-Website-Content-Needed.docx`.
> Both use the same numbers, so "item 6.1" means the same thing in each.

Thank you for the staff list and the client list. Both are now on the website: your six registered
valuers on the Our People page, and all your clients on the Our Clients page.

To finish the website, we need a few more things that only CMT can give us: some facts, some
photographs and a couple of decisions.

We never make anything up on the site. Where something is still missing, the site simply leaves it
out for now. As each item arrives, we add it straight away.

*Property listings are not in this checklist. The CMT team will add and update them directly through
the website's editing system.*

---

## How to use this checklist

1. Each item has its own number, for example **3.2**. Please mention the number when you send
   something, so we know what it is for.
2. Send things in any order, whenever they are ready. There is no need to wait until everything is
   complete.
3. If you are not sure of an answer, a best guess marked **"to confirm"** is very welcome. We will not
   publish it until you tell us it is right.

> **What the website says about CMT.** CMT is a valuation firm first and an estate agency second. For
> more than sixteen years, banks, public bodies and companies have trusted CMT when they need a
> property value they can rely on. Every figure comes from real market evidence, and every report is
> signed by a named, qualified valuer. If you feel any of this is not quite right, please let us know.

> **Tips for good photos.** Take photos in daylight, without flash. Hold the phone level, and turn it
> sideways for buildings and rooms. Tidy the space first. Please send the original files by email or
> a shared folder rather than through WhatsApp, which makes photos smaller and less sharp. Only
> photograph a client's building if they have agreed.
