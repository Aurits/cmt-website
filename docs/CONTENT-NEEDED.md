# What we need from CMT to finish the website

The new website is built. Every page works, on phones and on computers. What it is still missing is
information only CMT can give us: facts, numbers, photographs and a few decisions.

Wherever something is missing today, the site either leaves it out or says plainly that it is
coming. It never makes anything up. As each item below arrives, the matching part of the site
switches on.

---

## The story the website tells

So that every answer fits, this is the story every page is built around:

> **CMT is a valuation firm first and an estate agency second.** For more than sixteen years, banks,
> public bodies and companies have instructed CMT when they need a property figure they can rely
> on. Every figure starts from real market evidence, and every report is signed by a named,
> regulated valuer who answers for it.

If anything below would change that story, please tell us. It is much easier to adjust the story
now than after launch.

---

- [ ] **Real listings.** For each property: a title, the price (or rent), the area and town, the size,

- [ ] **Real photographs** of each property: at least five per property, taken in daylight.

- [ ] **The first two or three articles** for the Blog (optional at launch). For example: what a bank

## How to send things

Everything can come in one email or a shared folder, in any order. Photographs are best sent as
original files rather than through WhatsApp, which makes them smaller and blurrier. Where you are not
sure of an answer, a best guess marked *to confirm* is fine: we will keep it off the site until you
say it is right.
